Privacy Policy

MindSpark Uploader · The MindSpark Academy · Effective 1 November 2026

MindSpark Uploader is a private automation tool that publishes videos to The MindSpark Academy YouTube channel. It is operated by the channel owner and is used only with the channel owner's own Google account. It is not offered to the public, and no other person signs in to it.

What data the app accesses

The app requests a single Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. It uses this permission only to upload videos, with their titles, descriptions and tags, to the channel owner's YouTube channel. It does not read, collect or store any other data from Google or YouTube, such as email, contacts, viewing history, comments or analytics.

How data is stored

The only stored item is the OAuth refresh token that lets the app upload on the owner's behalf. It is kept as an encrypted secret in the owner's private GitHub repository and is used only by the owner's automated upload job.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share, transfer or disclose any Google user data to third parties, and does not use it for advertising or for training any machine-learning model.

Viewers of the channel

This app does not collect any information about people who watch the channel's videos. Viewing videos on YouTube is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Google API Services User Data Policy

MindSpark Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also complies with the YouTube Terms of Service.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions, after which the app can no longer upload anything.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. For questions, contact the channel owner through The MindSpark Academy's About page.
